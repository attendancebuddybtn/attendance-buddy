How it works

Three steps, about two minutes.

Install the app

Download the APK from this page and install it on your Android phone.

Log in once

Sign in with your university email inside the app. The login is kept, even after restarting your phone.

Allow on-time alerts

Tap Allow on the "Make alerts on time" card. From then on the app checks for you, even when it's closed.

Install on Android

The app isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks you to confirm the install once.

Install

  1. Tap Download for Android and open AttendanceBuddy.apk.
  2. If asked, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, go back and tap Install.
  3. Open Attendance Buddy and tap Log in.
  4. Sign in with your university email. The app opens your attendance by itself.

Get alerts exactly on time

  1. On the Make alerts on time card, tap Allow for Notifications, Alarms & reminders and Unrestricted battery.
  2. Samsung: Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Never sleeping apps → add Attendance Buddy.
Updating: the app tells you when a new version is out. Tap Update, download and install over the old app – your login stays.

On iPhone

The same app, free: you install it with your own Apple ID through SideStore or AltStore – no App Store and no payment. Or, without installing anything, add the reminders to your Calendar.

The app – free, with your Apple ID

  1. Install SideStore (it refreshes on the phone) or AltStore, following their guide. It needs a computer once and your free Apple ID.
  2. Turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode (the iPhone restarts).
  3. Tap Add to SideStore or Add to AltStore below, then install Attendance Buddy.
  4. Open it, allow notifications and log in with your university email.
Add to SideStore Add to AltStore
Free Apple IDs sign apps for 7 days at a time – SideStore / AltStore renew it for you, and show new versions as updates. Buttons not working? In SideStore / AltStore add this source: attendancebuddybtn.github.io/attendance-buddy/altstore.json. Using a computer? Download the .ipa for Sideloadly.

Or no install: Calendar reminders

  • 6:00 PM – punch out before you leave

Add to iPhone Calendar
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Add to iPhone Calendar.
  2. Tap Add All, choose a calendar and tap Done.
Holiday or leave day? Just dismiss the reminder. To change a time, open the event in Calendar, tap Edit, change it and choose Save for future events.

Optional: an alert when you leave campus

  1. Open the Shortcuts app → Automation → + → Leave.
  2. Choose your campus as the location, select Run Immediately and tap Next.
  3. Tap New Blank Automation → Add Action → Show Notification.
  4. Type Punch out on the biometric before you go and tap Done.
Needs iOS 17 or newer. Allow location access for Shortcuts when the iPhone asks.

Questions

Short answers to what people ask most.

Is my login or data sent anywhere?

No. You sign in inside the app, the same way as in a browser. The app only reads your own attendance page, and everything stays on your phone. There is no server and no account.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Android shows this for every app that isn't installed from the Play Store. Allow it once for your browser and install.

Alerts come late on my phone. What can I do?

Allow all three items on the "Make alerts on time" card. On Samsung, also add the app to Never sleeping apps (Settings → Battery → Background usage limits). Some phones pause apps to save battery unless you allow this.

Does it follow the attendance rules?

Yes, exactly as the portal shows them: on time between 06:00 and 10:00, 9 hours a day (8 hours minimum), the weekly target from Monday to Saturday, Week-Off and Holiday days, ULOP and LOP, and the leave deadline on the 20th.

Is there an iPhone app?

Yes, free. Apple lets anyone install apps on their own iPhone with a free Apple ID; SideStore and AltStore do that for you and renew it every 7 days (a free Apple ID can have 3 such apps). See On iPhone. iOS checks GITAM in the background less often than Android, so the app also sets the day's reminders in advance, and refreshes whenever you open it.

Does it show my leave requests?

Yes. Each time you open the app it reads your Leave, On Duty and Missing Biometric requests and shows their status – Pending at Supervisor, Pending at HOD or Approved – on that day. You get a notification when one moves on or is approved.

How do I update?

The app shows "New version available" on the Today screen. Tap Update, download from this page and install over the old app. Your login and history are kept.
